Italian Musical Terms

Copywork for Young Musicians

A 20-page landscape handwriting and vocabulary resource for young musicians, covering Italian musical terms commonly introduced in Grade 1 theory. Students copy each term and its meaning while becoming familiar with the vocabulary of dynamics, tempo, articulation, and musical expression.

Included Categories: Dynamics (piano, forte, cresc., decresc.), Tempo & Speed (Allegro, Andante, Adagio, rit., rall.), Character & Style (cantabile, dolce), Instructions (D.C., fine), and Articulations (legato, staccato).
