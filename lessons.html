Educational Approach

Tailored Musical Depth

Music theory, ear training, and historical context are naturally woven into every piano lesson at Acacia. For students and families who wish to pursue a particular area more deeply, concentrated 15-minute elective studies may be added according to the student's interests and needs.

15-Minute Elective Studies

Elective studies are concentrated 15-minute modules added to the weekly private lesson. Parents and students choose the area of study, preserving an individualized learning path.

What to Expect

Complimentary Introductory Lesson

New students are invited to a complimentary introductory lesson before beginning regular study. This gives us an opportunity to meet, discuss the student's experience and goals, and explore the piano together.

Individualized Study

Each student's repertoire and learning materials are selected according to their age, experience, interests, and developing abilities. Lessons may draw from different methods and materials rather than following a single curriculum from beginning to end.

A Musical Beginning

Beginners first spend several weeks becoming comfortable with the keyboard through rote pieces, trios, listening, improvisation, and musical games. Formal notation is introduced gradually as students connect what they hear with what they see and play.

Open Studio Policy

Parents are always welcome to stay and observe lessons. This open-studio policy supports transparent communication and allows families to be present as their child develops musical skills.
